About

Masha Cermak, a troubled teenager, lived a chaotic life alongside her sister Catherine Cermak. The two sisters lived unstable lives, the two of them and the way they would process trauma would drastically differ from each other.
Masha's life was turbulent from the instability of her environment her life would eventually take a downward spiral during her adolescence, culminating in uncertainty and lies.

All involved struggle individually with their own conflicts throughout while also being intertwined in the mess that is the tragic downfall of Masha Cermak.
