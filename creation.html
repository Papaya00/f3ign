    So... how did this come about?


    Fɘign initally began as an aimless group of characters in 2020, initally starting as just one character.
    Though there was character depth, there was no particular direction that was set at the time, no plot, no storyline.

    The project initally had a much more light-hearted tone before changes in writing darrasticaly changed, this was due to advancements in general worldbuilding/storytelling/whatever.

    The project was a way to escape from the world during the height of the pandemic, though, once things became less chaotic in that regard, writing decisions shifted.
    A quick thing to note is that this project is mostly complete in terms of writing but, has a lot of work to be done relating to it (comics, art, other stuff)

    What else?

    This section contains both info about the creation and part of the story itself.

    • - At the very beginning it was supposed to take place in current time,
      even origianally having a time travel aspect to it.

    • - All of my characters are colour coded, each of them have an assigned colour
      (example: Quinn's assigned colour is light purple)

    • - Masha was created 5 days before my birthday! (June 30)

    • - I base a lot of characters off music genres, a lot being based off of :
      grunge, industrial, nu-metal (sorry lol) along with a bit of crust and death metal.

    • - Part of the story extends after the main time period, going as far as current day.
