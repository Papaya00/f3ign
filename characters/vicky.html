Gender: Female

Nationality: Ukrainian-American


Victoria 'Vicky' Bonacelli was born in western Ukraine in 1985 before being adopted by a family in the United States. She was moved to Colorado in 1988 at the age of 3.

TEEN YEARS

Growing up and being told by her parents that she could do more and try harder at any accomplishment, she became an over achiever. Her parents didn't consider the potential consequences that their actions could have on their daughter.

Frustrated and burnt out, she started rebelling in any way she could think of. Wearing low cut shirts, dying her hair red, smoking, drinking, and generally anything her parents didn't approve of.

Vicky was forced into a program that her parents placed her in after catching wind of her rebellious behavior. This led to her committing her life to christ and changing her ways, even avoiding secular music.

RELATIONSHIPS

In terms of relationships, she was in plenty short-term ones. Most were men though, excluding one.

Masha, a good friend of Vicky started to fall in love with her. Eventually, Masha both simultaneously came out and confessed her feelings for Vicky.
Being presented with all this information including that one of her closer friends had a crush on her caused Vicky feel conflicted about her own feelings.

After being asked whether she wanted to be with her, she accepted, initally just to expereince being with another girl though, eventually started genuinely fall in love with her.

THE INCIDENT

After one year of dating, Vicky became withdrawn. Shortly after her Christian awakening, she couldn't figure out how to tell Masha about her life-style change. Upon telling her, she was later bombarded with visits from Masha begging for them to get back together but, her relationship with christ was more important.

One day when talking to a group of friends, recalling her past relationships, she let slip that she was with a girl. To them, this was a huge deal since Vicky was straight and only ever with men. They asked her who but, she refused to tell until she was pestered enough.

It was Masha.

Things got quiet. They didn't expect her to be with Masha out of all people. They were seen as polar opposites.

With this information, rumors spread and Masha started recieving harassment.
She felt guilt of how Masha started to be treated and how much hate Masha had for her. Masha was being harassed relentlessly indirectly due to Vicky's slip up.

APPEARANCE

Red-ish hair parted in a zig-zag (her natuaral hair color is light brown), grey eyes, some acne scars along with some freckles, wearing a off white and black striped sweater, lightly distressed blue jeans, and red converse.
