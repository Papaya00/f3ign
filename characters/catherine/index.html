Catherine Palenkova Cermak was born in poor urban Russia in March of 1983 to her (at the time) 17-year-old mother Olga and her 24-year-old father [REDACTED].

CHILDHOOD

Growing up in lower class urban Russia with a teenage mother and a predatory father, she had quite a rough upbringing. Catherine started exhibiting concerning behavior around the time her younger sister was born. Catherine often pinched and did anything to make her cry, thinking it was amusing, though, her mother certainly didn't think so.

During childhood after a head injury, she started showing concerning behavior and becoming facisnated with violence and causing pain to others and herself on occasions.

Her mother would take her to a number of child psychologists try to get Catherine a diagnosis. Though, most appointments didn't go anywhere, Catherine refused to speak to them.

After years without intervention from psychologists, her mother would eventually find out one of the potential causes aside from her previous concussion.

For years, Catherine had been repeatedly raped by her father, giving an explanation to some of her avoidant and anxious behavior.

The hatred of her younger sister stemmed from a 5-year-old Masha witnessing the abuse and not understanding what was happening or doing anything to stop it, in turn, seeing her just as bad as their father.

At the age of 11, her family immigrated to the United States in 1994

ADOLESCENCE

Catherine initially aspired to be a poet but that pivoted to wanting to be an activist.

Catherine became jealous of Masha because of how likable and easy going she was.
Catherine was not well liked because of her general attitude and other people thinking she was weird. Catherine would be mocked by her peers for her thick accent and her attempts to defend herself against their remarks to no avail.

High school would be when she would gain a close group of friends. Catherine would meet two girls from a school club she was in.

Catherine became more respected in the girls' advocacy group she was in and used this as an opportunity to spread hatred for her sister.

Catherine hated her younger sister to an obsessive degree to the point of trying to ruin her life in every way possible. Afterall, she was already beating her and psychologically torturing her, why not involve everyone else too?

SISTERHOOD

Catherine was often described as a "devil spawn" by her younger sister and simply "scary" by the youngest. Despite these traits given to her, she was often given chances to be a better person to no avail as she would always do something that would make those no longer trust her.

The relationship between Catherine and her two sisters has never been the best but there were some redeeming traits depending on the circumstances.

Catherine was very literate and would often correct people on their grammer unprompted, ironic since mostly speaks Russian and not English but, that doesn't mean she doesn't know what she's talking about.

ADULTHOOD

Catherine's mental issues worsened as a young adult and became more aggressive towards her family and Masha especially, often beating her to a pulp.

Being an adult, Catherine was very immature. Catherine went up to one of her peers after rumors spread about Masha's supposedly perverted behavior, paying this person over 200 dollars to spread elaborate rumors.

On the day of her sister's killing spree, Catherine would be shot in the shoulder and becoming partially deaf in one ear. While bleeding out, she would hear a loud thump. After waking up in the hospital, the press would flock to her, despraretly wanting answers for what happened.

MEDIA

Catherine was one of 4 people who was shot, her being the only injury. This specific case would be a huge deal due to the murderer being a teenage girl with a lengthy internet presence, also being the first murder in the area in over 35 years.

With the amount of pity and attention, Catherine would revel in the amount of attention from the local press (and eventually national for a short period).
She took this oppritunity to share her expereince with having Masha Cermak as her younger sister, remarking how bad their relationship was and going on about her mental issues.

The events depicted were convincing to most and believed her without question, those who did question her story such as inconsistencies with certian details, they would get dogpilled by those accusing them as being a sympathizer with a drug addicted teenaged murderer.
