Dill Dillon, full name being Wallace Charles Dillon III, was born in South Carolina (U.S). With him would come with his younger sister Nicole in late 1990. Nicole was born with several medical issues that had to be accommodated for, indirectly leading to him being neglected.

At 12, he would end up moving to Koval, Colorado (U.S) in 1998.

ADOLESCENCE

As a teenager, Dill was a seemingly laid back, calm and leveled headed person, though struggled immensely at home and internally. Feeling ignored and over looked.

At 13 Dill's parents deemed him perfectly able fully care for his sister and medically assisting her. Dill and Nicole had a normal sibling dynamic, including some banter but, he started to feel genuine resentment as a result of having all of these responsibilities.

Dill had gradually become depressed, angry and nihilistic. He started taking sleeping pills as a form of escapism but often had horrific nightmares as a result. Months after he started indulging in sleeping pills, started vomiting, which worried him and eventually motivated him to stop. Dill eventually moved on to marijuana which made his life a little more bearable.

2002 would be when his life would change for the worst. Nicole would suffer a brain hemorrhage of an indeterminate cause.

Dill was there at the time of the incident. A part of him thought it was more merciful for her to die now than live a life surrounded in medical equipment and expenses. After 10 minutes, the ambulance was called, she was taken to a hospital where she would spend a week on life support.

His parents (especially his mother) initially accused him of murdering her, though, this was out of grief and shock. Weeks after, which later shifted to accusing him of being lazy and negligent.

Dill would forget most of what happened on that day due to his already declining mental state. He disassociated for 5 months and went into a fugue state. His parents didn't help with this at all. They insisted his condition was attention seeking. He was reluctantly taken to a psychologist where they did a trauma assessment on him. He was later diagnosed with PTSD.

Dill, still grieving with the death of his sister, he would yet have to struggle with another loss.

Dill met Masha in the fall of 1998 during 7th grade. They became close friends quickly and hung out together almost daily. She was like another sister to him. He didn't take her death well either, it took over a year before he could somewhat move on.

ADULTHOOD

Dill got his own place and a shit paying job. His personal life was nothing much to speak of, just a young adult trying to get by.

After the crimes that took place on the day of Masha's death, he often took Masha's side, defending her and made frequent attempts to humanize her, some did listen, most insisted that she was still a murderer regardless and unworthy of any consideration.

Rumors were often stated as fact and no one questioned any of them, she was a bad person, anything bad about a bad person is always, 100% true.

While he was trying to sway the public's perception of a dead teenager who went postal, he found a boyfriend.

Dill started dating one of his closest friends, Octavius back in 2004. They were off and on, sometimes breaking up because of external circumstances or thinking that they're not gay. They were both pretty happy together either way, gay or not.
